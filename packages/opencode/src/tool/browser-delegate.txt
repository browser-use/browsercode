Delegate one short, obvious browser state transition to a cheap Browser Use leaf agent.

Use this tool only when:
- the current operation is narrow and browser-only;
- the stopping condition is directly observable on the page;
- roughly eight or fewer model steps should be sufficient;
- the parent can verify the resulting page afterward.

Good delegations include filling a bounded form, applying known filters,
navigating to a known record, or revealing a known result. Do not delegate
broad research, exhaustive scraping, final-answer composition, filesystem
work, or ambiguous interpretation.

For a qualifying transition, delegate before trying to implement the same
interaction with browser_execute. Do not save this tool as a last-ditch retry
after many manual attempts. If the leaf gives up, take over with
browser_execute; do not repeatedly delegate the same transition.

The leaf agent knows it is a subagent and may give up. Its completion status is
only a claim. After this tool returns, ALWAYS use browser_execute to inspect the
browser and independently verify done_when before relying on the result.
