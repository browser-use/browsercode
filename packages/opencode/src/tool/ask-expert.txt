Forks the complete current conversation to a stronger BrowserCode model. The
expert shares the exact live CDP session and workspace, can act in the browser,
and returns control here with a compact receipt and fresh browser state.

Use it as soon as strategy becomes unclear, the page behaves unexpectedly, a
source or record must be substituted, progress stalls, or an important claim is
not supported by evidence. Also use it before the first final answer to audit
the original requirements, coverage, required fields, calculations, artifacts,
and evidence. If a previous expert call already performed that final audit, do
not call again merely for ceremony.

Obvious clicks, typing, scrolling, and already-clear browser sequences do not
need an early handoff. Giving up early is good: ask the expert instead of
repeating a weak strategy. The `request` should state only what is uncertain or
what must be checked; the full conversation is inherited automatically.
