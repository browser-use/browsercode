Runs a cheap Browser Use subagent on the current CDP tab. It repeatedly sees a
compact page state—URL, visible text, numbered interactive elements, and a
screenshot when needed—and emits up to three click/type/scroll/read actions.

Delegate one complete episode when its start is known, its result is visible,
and it should finish within 25 steps: for example a form, filters plus bounded
results, or a known finite list. Keep open research, completeness or source
judgment, debugging/CDP/API work, files, and authentication recovery here.

Put every known input in `task`. Make `done_when` name the exact visible state
and values to return; the subagent must observe rather than infer them. It may
give up. On success, use its returned actions, values, final state, and attached
screenshot directly. On failure, take over and do not delegate this tab again.
